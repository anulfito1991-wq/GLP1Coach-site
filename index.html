What it does

  • Log injections with 8-site rotation, dose, and notes
  • Track side effects, weight, protein, and water
  • Pull weight + nutrition from Apple Health, free
  • Weekly Coach Summary written on your device by Apple Intelligence
  • Plateau detection + prescriber prompt sheet

Privacy first

Nothing leaves your phone. No accounts, no cloud sync, no analytics, no ads. The AI runs entirely on your device using Apple's on-device language model.
