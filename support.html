Support

How do I add my first medication?

Open the app, complete the brief onboarding (medication name, dose, frequency, start date), and your Today screen will start counting down to your next shot.

How do I connect Apple Health?

Go to Settings → Apple Health → Sync now the first time. iOS will ask which categories you want to share. You can adjust permissions any time at iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → GLP-1 Coach.

Why don't I see Coach summaries?

Coach summaries require an active GLP-1 Coach Pro subscription. The Insights tab will show a teaser and a free 7-day trial offer.

On devices that support Apple Intelligence (iOS 26+ on supported hardware), summaries are written by Apple's on-device language model. On older devices, GLP-1 Coach uses on-device rule-based summaries — same data, simpler language.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open iPhone Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → GLP-1 Coach Pro to cancel or change plan. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period.

I deleted the app — can I restore my data?

If you have iCloud device backup enabled and reinstall the app on the same Apple ID before your next iCloud backup completes, your data may be restored from backup. If not, the data is gone — GLP-1 Coach does not store your data on any server. For Pro subscribers, your subscription is restored via Settings → Restore purchases.

Is the Coach giving me medical advice?

No. Every Coach output is framed as a personal reflection of patterns in your own logs. It is not medical or dosing advice. Always discuss your treatment with your prescriber.
